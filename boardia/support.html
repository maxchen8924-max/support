常見問題

是否需要登入？

不需要。當前版本不要求註冊帳號。

是否包含廣告或內購？

不包含廣告，不包含遊戲內商店，不包含內購。

棋局資料儲存在哪裡？

當前版本的棋局進度和偏好設定主要儲存在本機裝置上。刪除 App 會清除這些本機資料。

圍棋和陸戰棋是否是專業賽事規則？

當前版本更偏休閒和入門體驗，不宣稱專業賽事級規則或高階棋力。

Support

If you need help with Boardia, email longchen8924@gmail.com or use the developer contact method in the app store. Please include your device model, operating system version, app version, affected game, steps to reproduce the issue, and screenshots or a screen recording when possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to sign in?

No. The current version does not require an account.

Does the app include ads or in-app purchases?

No. The app does not include ads, an in-game store, or in-app purchases.

Where is game data stored?

Game progress and preferences are mainly stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Are Go and Army Chess implemented as professional tournament rules?

The current version is designed for casual and introductory play. It does not claim professional tournament-level rules or advanced playing strength.
