這裡棋很多隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 6 月 11 日

1. 我們收集哪些資訊

這裡棋很多是一款離線棋類遊戲合輯。當前版本不要求註冊帳號，不包含廣告，不包含內購，不接入第三方分析 SDK，也不主動收集可識別個人身分的資訊。

應用程式會在裝置本機儲存遊戲設定、未完成棋局進度和應用程式偏好設定。這些資料儲存在你的裝置上，用於恢復對局和改善本機體驗。當前版本不會把這些資料上傳到我們的伺服器。

2. 權限和裝置資料

當前版本不要求相機、麥克風、通訊錄、定位、照片、藍牙、通知等敏感權限。

3. 第三方服務

當前版本不包含廣告 SDK、支付 SDK、統計分析 SDK 或社群分享 SDK。如果未來版本接入第三方服務，我們會在發布前更新本政策，並在應用商店後台同步更新資料收集說明。

4. 兒童隱私

當前版本不面向兒童收集個人資訊。若你認為應用程式中存在兒童個人資訊相關問題，請透過支援頁面、支援信箱或應用商店頁面的開發者聯絡方式聯絡我們。

5. 資料刪除

當前版本的資料主要儲存在本機裝置中。你可以透過刪除 App 來清除本機儲存的棋局和設定資料。

6. 聯絡我們

如有隱私相關問題，請寄信至 longchen8924@gmail.com，或透過本網站的支援頁面、應用商店頁面的開發者聯絡方式聯絡我們。

7. 政策更新

我們可能會根據功能變化或法律要求更新本政策。更新後的政策會透過應用商店頁面或支援頁面提供。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Boardia is an offline collection of board games. The current version does not require an account, display ads, offer in-app purchases, integrate third-party analytics SDKs, or actively collect personally identifiable information.

The app stores game settings, unfinished game progress, and app preferences locally on your device. This information is used to restore games and improve the on-device experience. The current version does not upload this information to our servers.

2. Permissions and Device Data

The current version does not require sensitive permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, Bluetooth, or notifications.

3. Third-Party Services

The current version does not include advertising, payment, analytics, or social-sharing SDKs. If a future version adds a third-party service, we will update this policy before release and update the related data-collection disclosure in the app store console.

4. Children's Privacy

The current version does not collect personal information from children. If you believe the app contains an issue involving a child's personal information, please contact us through the support page, support email, or the developer contact method shown in the app store.

5. Data Deletion

The current version stores its data mainly on your device. You can delete the app to remove locally stored games and settings.

6. Contact Us

For privacy questions, email longchen8924@gmail.com, use this website's support page, or use the developer contact method shown in the app store.

  • Developer/entity: Long Chen (as displayed by the app store developer account)
  • Support email: longchen8924@gmail.com
  • Support page: support.html

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy in response to feature changes or legal requirements. Updated versions will be available through the app store listing or support page.
